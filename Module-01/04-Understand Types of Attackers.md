# Understand Types of Attackers

**Attackers** are individuals or groups that attempt to gain unauthorized access to systems, steal information, cause damage, or disrupt services.

## a. Script Kiddies

**Script kiddies** are inexperienced attackers who use **existing tools, scripts, or exploits created by others** without fully understanding how they work.

```text id="w2h8h7"
Existing Tool
     ↓
Script Kiddie
     ↓
Target
```

Example:

> A beginner downloads an existing attack tool and uses it against a target without understanding the underlying technology.

They may cause damage accidentally because they do not fully understand the tools they are using.

**Easy idea:**

> **Script Kiddie = Uses existing hacking tools without much understanding.**

---

## b. Cyber Criminals

**Cyber criminals** are individuals or groups who use cyber attacks primarily for **financial or personal gain**.

They may:

* Steal money
* Steal personal information
* Deploy ransomware
* Sell stolen data
* Commit online fraud
* Take over accounts

```text id="9f7qj4"
Cyber Criminal
      ↓
Cyber Attack
      ↓
Money / Data
      ↓
Financial Gain
```

Example:

> A criminal group steals customer information and sells it or uses it for fraud.

**Easy idea:**

> **Cyber Criminal = Uses cyber attacks for illegal financial or personal gain.**

---

## c. Insider Threats

An **insider threat** comes from someone who already has **legitimate access to an organization's systems or information**.

An insider threat can be:

* A malicious employee
* A careless employee
* A contractor
* A compromised employee account

```text id="l2p7k3"
Insider
   ↓
Legitimate Access
   ↓
Misuse / Compromise
   ↓
Security Incident
```

Example:

> An employee intentionally copies confidential company data and gives it to an unauthorized person.

Insider threats do not always involve intentional actions. An employee can accidentally expose sensitive information.

**Easy idea:**

> **Insider Threat = Security threat involving someone with legitimate access.**

---

## d. Hacktivists

**Hacktivists** are individuals or groups that use cyber attacks to promote **political, social, or ideological causes**.

They may target organizations or websites to attract attention to their cause.

Common activities can include:

* Website disruption
* Data leaks
* Website defacement
* Information campaigns

```text id="e7w2q4"
Hacktivist
    ↓
Cyber Attack
    ↓
Target
    ↓
Political / Social Message
```

Example:

> A group attacks a website to draw attention to a political or social issue.

**Easy idea:**

> **Hacktivist = Uses cyber activity to promote a cause or ideology.**

---

## e. Nation-State Actors

**Nation-state actors** are attackers who are **supported, directed, or sponsored by a government**.

They may target:

* Government organizations
* Military systems
* Critical infrastructure
* Technology companies
* Research organizations
* Other countries

Their goals can include:

* Espionage
* Intelligence gathering
* Disruption
* Strategic advantage

```text id="f5d7k2"
Government / State
       ↓
Nation-State Actor
       ↓
Target
       ↓
Espionage / Disruption
```

Example:

> A government-backed group conducts cyber espionage against another country's government systems to gather intelligence.

**Easy idea:**

> **Nation-State Actor = Government-backed cyber attacker.**
